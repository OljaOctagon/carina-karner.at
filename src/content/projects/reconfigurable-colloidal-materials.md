# Reconfigurable Colloidal Materials

Designing responsive structures that reorganize under changing thermodynamic conditions.

This project explores how colloidal particles can be engineered to form structures that adapt, reconfigure, and transform in response to external stimuli. By combining particle-based simulations with statistical mechanical analysis, we investigate the design rules that enable colloidal materials to switch between distinct structural states.
