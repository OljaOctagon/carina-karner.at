# Partially Bonded Crystals

Nanocrystals can self-assemble into ordered superstructures, forming functional solids that may inherit the electronic properties of individual nanocrystals. This project investigates how partial bonding between nanocrystals leads to porous and polymorphic crystal structures.

## Key Results

- Identified pathways to porous crystal phases through partial bonding
- Characterized polymorphic transitions in 3D nanocrystal solids
- Combined particle-based simulation with experimental SAXS data

## Publication

Karner, C. & Bianchi, E. "Partially bonded crystals: a pathway to porosity and polymorphism." *ACS Nano*, 19, 5146 (2025).
