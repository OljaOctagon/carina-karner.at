# Magnetic Patchy Particles

Self-assembly beyond rings and chains.

This project investigates the self-assembly of magnetic patchy particles --- particles with spatially defined magnetic interactions --- and how their directional bonding leads to complex structures beyond simple rings and chains. We use Monte Carlo simulations and coarse-grained models to map out the phase behavior and identify design principles for programmable magnetic colloids.
